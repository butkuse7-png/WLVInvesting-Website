FAQ

Common questions about multifamily investing, syndications, and what to expect with WLV Investing.

What is a multifamily syndication?

A syndication is a structure where multiple investors pool capital to acquire a property. Typically, a sponsor team sources the deal, arranges financing, manages operations, and provides reporting.

What’s the difference between a syndication and a joint venture?

In a syndication, passive investors typically participate as limited partners. In a joint venture, partners often share more direct involvement, roles, and decision-making—based on the agreement.

Do I need to be accredited to invest?

It depends on the offering structure. Some opportunities may be open to non-accredited investors, while others may be limited to accredited investors only.

How do you manage risk?

We focus on conservative underwriting, stress-testing scenarios, and operational execution that prioritizes durable cash flow and downside protection. Every investment carries risk, and we aim to make risks clear.

How often will I receive updates?

Update frequency can vary by deal, but our goal is consistent, predictable reporting with clear performance metrics and progress on the business plan.
