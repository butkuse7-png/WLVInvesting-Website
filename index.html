FAQ

Common questions about multifamily investing, syndications, and what to expect with WLV Investing.

What is a multifamily syndication?

A syndication is where multiple investors pool capital to acquire a property. A sponsor team manages acquisition, operations, and reporting.

Do I need to be accredited to invest?

It depends on the offering structure. Some opportunities may be limited to accredited investors.

How do you manage risk?

We focus on conservative underwriting, stress-testing scenarios, and risk controls like reserves and disciplined operations.

How often will I receive updates?

Update frequency varies by deal, but our goal is consistent reporting with clear metrics and progress updates.
